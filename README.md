## Hi there, I'm Ema 👋

I am a cross-ecosystem ecologist studying how spatial flows connect ecosystems and shape biodiversity and ecosystem functioning. I integrate fieldwork, experiments, synthesis, and modelling to address questions across scales. I am passionate about developing reproducible and reusable analytical workflows for ecology, promoting open science, and science communication.

I am currently a postdoc in the lab of Prof Jana Isanta-Navarro at the University of Copenhagen. 

Know more about my research by visiting my [website](https://emanuele-giacomuzzo.github.io/website/).
